# Native memory protection with corroborated revision

**Measured:** two separately grounded source cohorts now regulate whether resident
student memories revise their connections. Disagreement leaves student RAM intact;
corroboration admits native learning. The network both protects existing facts and
accepts legitimate changes and new facts in this designed associative-memory task.

This advances the previous learned-source transfer/revision result: a correcting
teacher could revise students, but a wrong teacher could also corrupt them. This
study inserts a native agreement requirement and tests the protection/update tradeoff.
The agreement rule is fixed and engineered. The source associations, competing
student records and their binary connection changes are learned on the card.

## Important boundaries, also measured

**Waiting has a cost.** When just source A receives a legitimate reversal, native
students retain the old association:432/864 total and0/432 affected-cue recall.
After B receives the change, transfer restores864/864. The circuit detects agreement,
not which source is right. Pending/stale answers are reported separately from success.

**Learning remains available.** A separate one-cycle cell then introduces two new
facts. All three initializations pass96/96 six-fact exams, totaling288/288 while
retaining the original four facts. This is a six-association capacity check, not
maximum memory capacity or general importance-aware eviction.

**Agreement is not truth.** When both sources receive the same false fact, the
network accepts it. Across three cells, recall is72/144 overall and0/72 on affected
cues; unchanged cues remain72/72. CPU agreement fails in the same way. This expected
scientific negative is preserved despite valid execution and exact readbacks.

precedes every physical cell. All44 saved CPU/native streams pass the independent

SRAM carry pointer, mode journal and complete three-bank RAM boundary is checked.
Fixed route/helper RAM stays unchanged. Student transfer labels remain zero, with

## Hardware and claim scope

Seven sequential FNP3 placements; three plastic banks,48 plastic and272 fixed helper
records plus four fixed coding/routing stages. The two source cohorts share a single
four-class NPU bank, rather than being two independently clocked NPUs. Independent
class-partition updates are verified; simultaneous execution is not inferred.

The predicate is qualified for at most one responding record per source class,
checked on every native query/transfer. General duplicate-match robustness is open.
Representation, evidence, complementary retirement examples, agreement and role
schedule are engineered. Facts are synthetic cues, not unattended sensor knowledge.
No autonomous scheduler, source-reliability learning, truth discovery, power-loss
persistence, chip-wide utilization or calibrated energy/speed advantage is established.
Strong CPU implementations also perform this function.

The film shows all48 plastic records and their actual stored input-connection masks.
Returned source class flags and student record responses are measured; per-record
source dots and hidden feature activations are derived from saved state. Fixed
helper records are summarized rather than drawn. Camera, positions, glow and slowed
timing are schematic; this is recorded chip replay, not live electrical spike capture.
