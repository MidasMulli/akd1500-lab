## 2. Separate compute capacity from shared mesh capacity

compiler-constrained placements. Compare same-node and cross-node partitions

Primary endpoints: exact outputs, physical memory isolation, monitored service
cost, total host completion and burst throughput. Compare isolation and mixed
load. Test alternate A/B ordering to expose ordering-dependent state effects.
